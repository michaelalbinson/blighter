- Why goals for engineers die after week two
  - Most big goals fail because delay is huge and expectancy is low
- SMART = specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time bound
- CSI = challenging, specific, immediate, and approach oriented
- Top down goals work best when the outcome is clear
- Top down goals still fail if you turn them into rigid contracts
- When I do not know how to make a SMART goal, I go with a short experiment
  - Tiny experiments map great to the motivation equation
