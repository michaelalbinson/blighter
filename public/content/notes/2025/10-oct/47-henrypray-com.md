- Labor Efficiency: The Obvious First Layer
  - agents are cheaper than people
  - let agents do grunt work first, level up your people
- Net-New Revenue: The Second Layer
  - backlog of stuff you never did. Too manual. Too small. Too annoying. Until now.
- Optimization: The Golden Layer
  - LLMs bring decision fluency - understand context, nuance, and ambiguity
  - ML brings decision precision
