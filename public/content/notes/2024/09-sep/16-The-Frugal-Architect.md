- make cost a non-functional requirement
- systems that last align cost to business needs
- architecting is a series of tradeoffs - revisit them regularly
- unobserved systems lead to unknown costs
- cost aware architectures implement cost controls
- cost optimization is incremental
- unchallenged success leads to assumptions - continuously question what worked in the past
