- Every talk should start with an AIM: Audience, Intent, Message
  - Who are the audience for the talk? What do you intend to achieve by giving the presentation?
- Try to include at least one STAR moment—Something They’ll Always Remember
- Presentations are most interesting if they are structured with contrasts
- The human brain is incredibly attuned to stories
- When giving a talk, position your audience as the hero - call them to adventure from safety
