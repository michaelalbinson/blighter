- What engineering taste actually is
- immature engineers are too inflexible about their taste
- your engineering taste is composed of the set of engineering values you find most important
  - resiliency
  - speed
  - readability
  - corectness
  - flexibility
  - portability
  - development speed
- how to ID bad taste
  - bad taste is inflexible
  - focus on flexibility to grow good taste
