- If you offload the core of your job to AI, don't be surprised when someone decides AI can do it instead
- without going through the motions of a task, you don't develop _taste_
- you need intuition for something to be good at it
- Use AI to augment your work, not replace it
  - combine your unique insight and creativity with the automation firepower from models
- pick a recurring, manual process to automate instead of trying to tackle an entire function or workflow
