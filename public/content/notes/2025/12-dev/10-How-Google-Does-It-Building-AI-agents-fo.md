- Hands-on learning builds trust
  - For people to trust technology, they need to understand it
  - incrementally introduce the team to show it positive outcomes
- Prioritize real problems, not just possibilities
  - initial use cases focused on building confidence in security agents and validating their effectiveness before tackling more complex problems
  - Distillation - reducing large amounts of data to summarize patterns across security tickets
- Measure, evaluate, and iterate to successfully scale
  - eliminate repetitive tasks
- Get your foundations right
  - we assign a persona (“who are you?”) and identity (“what do we expect from you?”) for each agent
  - assign a specific sphere of responsibility with a defined scope of what an agent can do
  - AI agents are designed to operate based on a limited window of context and knowledge
  - helps manage risk more effectively
  - Quality agents provide oversight - ensure primary agents are aligned with standards
