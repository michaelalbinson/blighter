1. Start Small, Stay Small (with models)
2. Design for Production from Day Zero
  - Logging, monitoring, versioning, and guardrails 
3. Optimize for Repeatability and Model Improvement, Not Novelty
4. Think in Feedback Loops
5. Solve Pain, Not to Impress
6. Embed Cost and Risk Awareness Early
  - Track unit economics from day one
  - Benchmark small vs. large models. Cloud vs. local execution
7. Make Ownership Clear
8. Control Costs Upfront
9. Involve Users From Day Zero

tl;dr: Stop treating AI POCs as disposable demos. Treat them as the first draft of a production system.
