- what doesn't work
  - micromanaging
  - public criticism, framed as feedback
  - silence
  - jumping in to help too fast
  - inconsistent expectations
- things to do
  - praise in public, criticize in private
  - give people autonomy - set guardrails, then step back
  - ask before jumping in
  - define what good looks like
  - replace silence with signals
  - encourage people
  - consult people to show them you value your input
  - ask your people to introspect
