different perspectives on why people started their engineering blogs

- it's a cheat code it is to have a public body of work that people can just passively stumble upon
- focus my blog on the stuff that was missing from the documentation: odd corners of the language, why we’d made certain design decisions over others, that sort of thing
- blogging was part of maintaining your online identity in ye olde days
- my focus on writing shifted from “writing about zany explorations” to “writing as a means for teaching myself a topic, or solidifying my understanding of the topic.”
