- as we rise up the org chart, strategy is the job
- first rule: strategy is contextual
- failure mode: applying one strategy to another context without considering differences
- second rule of strategy: timeframe varies with the level of uncertainty you’re navigating.
  - Proximate objectives chart your path
- strategy is about defining the incremental steps – the proximate objectives – that can take us towards that end state
- Strategy is understanding where we are at – context – and the path from there to where we need to go
- four things for strategy:
  - time - energy - to think deeply about it
  - context to situate it
  - direction to identify proximate objectives
  - expertise to chart the path
- Execution is when the strategy becomes real.
- Product strategy drives your proximate objectives.
- Technical strategy evolves the context
- Your team strategy must be grounded in execution
- The you as a person strategy requires that you carve out time to be strategic
