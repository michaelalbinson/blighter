- why people don't hire juniors
  - In small startups - “we are a very small team and we don’t have time to mentor juniors, we need engineers who will be very productive from day 1"
  - In medium-sized companies - “we are going to grow very fast, we need engineers who can handle scale and have faced such challenges before"
  - In big companies - “our infrastructure is super complex, it’ll take juniors too long to ramp up".
- what companies miss
  - old assumptions about onboarding time
  - outdated interviews
  - they don't know where to find good junionrs
- why you can enjoy working with juniors
  - not restricted by what they know
  - learn fast and search for feedback
  - higher ceiling
  - fresh energy
- how to hire
  - find the right mindset (look for passion, curiosity, openness)
  - "use whatever you want" home assignments - then walk solution together
  - problem solving without AI
  - evaluate their AI strategy
- after-hire
  - invest in mentoring infra
  - be patient with short-term productivity
  - measure the right metrics
  - talk to them
  - start small
