- Goals are a communication tool - to keep people aligned on what matters
  - goal-setting is a top-down exercise - start at the company level
  - company size and stage matter - running a more established business, six-month goals can work well, seed, every two months
- rules
  - No Company Needs More Than Three Company Goals
    - Before you set goals, you should be able to draw your business on one page
  - One Goal Needs to Win in a Fight
    - If we do nothing else, what matters most?
  - Explain It to Me Like I’m Five
    - can the intern who started on Monday read your goals and know how to use them?
  - Strategy Should Hurt
    - Prioritization — real prioritization — should be painful
    - document non-goals clearly as well, which protect focus
  - One Goal. One Owner
  - Goals Alone Are Not Enough
    - Goals are a map, be prepared to revise it/course correct as needed
