- Empower people to stop the line (or at least part of it) when they notice a problem.
- Set clear expectations for who should fix problems in which timeframe.
- Ensure that the postmortem leads to concrete actions with owners and deadlines.
- Implement permanent fixes that prevent the same issues from ever happening again.
