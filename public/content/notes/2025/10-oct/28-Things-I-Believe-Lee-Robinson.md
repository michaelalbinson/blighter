- Shipping fast beats the best strategy
- You have no career ceiling (Grit > talent; no substitute for putting in the hours)
- Be ruthlessly truth seeking (Have strong opinions, loosely held)
- Communication is the job (Clear writing is clear thinking, everyone needs to be a better writer)
  - take notes, even if it's just for yourself
- Education is the best form of developer marketing
- Leadership means owning outcomes beyond the org chart (Influence > titles)
- Work can also be your hobby (n.b., ick)
- Demos > memos (You could have built a prototype in v0 during the meeting)
- Hiring is what separates good leaders from great (There are two hiring answers: hell yes or no; Hire people you can learn from)
- Always try to assume good intent
