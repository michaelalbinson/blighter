- agentic browsers have too much of your data
  - auth'd sessions
  - browser storage access
  - local filesystem access
  - device hardware integration
  - full execution env control
- Recommended Security Principles
  - strict sandboxing on a per-task basis
  - granular user consent for high risk actions
  - principle of least priv
  - transparent and auditable reasoning
  - user intention alignment
